GENERATIVE AI · FINANCIAL RISK

Automated Adversarial Stress Testing Using Generative Adversarial Networks for Dynamic Risk Scenario Synthesis

Builds a three-part framework: scenario generation, adversarial stress amplification, and risk propagation. The study uses historical financial indicators to synthesize severe, previously unseen stress trajectories.

REINFORCEMENT LEARNING · TRADE EXECUTION

Reinforcement Learning for Liquidity-Weighted Optimal Trade Execution Strategies

Introduces a liquidity-weighted reinforcement-learning execution framework using order-book imbalance, market impact, slippage, and execution risk. The paper evaluates the approach against TWAP, VWAP, Almgren–Chriss, and baseline RL strategies in simulation.
