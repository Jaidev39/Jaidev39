RESEARCH

Applied AI and quantitative research.

01
GENERATIVE AI · FINANCIAL RISK

Automated Adversarial Stress Testing Using Generative Adversarial Networks for Dynamic Risk Scenario Synthesis

This work builds a three-part framework for scenario generation, adversarial stress amplification, and risk propagation using historical financial indicators to synthesize severe, previously unobserved risk states.

Methods: GAN-based scenario generation; adversarial perturbation; systemic risk modeling.
Verified findings: Dynamic stress scenarios can expand the risk surface and support stronger scenario testing.
Contribution: Framed the system architecture and experimental analysis for dynamic stress synthesis.

02
REINFORCEMENT LEARNING · TRADE EXECUTION

Reinforcement Learning for Liquidity-Weighted Optimal Trade Execution Strategies

The paper introduces a liquidity-weighted reinforcement-learning execution framework centered on order-book imbalance, market impact, slippage, execution risk, and policy learning for trade optimization.

Methods: PPO / DQN framing; liquidity-aware reward; execution optimization.
Verified findings: Execution outcomes improve when the policy incorporates liquidity dynamics and market impact.
Contribution: Designed the execution policy framing and quantitative experiments for the study.
